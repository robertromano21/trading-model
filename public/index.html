        Model settings and conventions

        Breakouts use both SMA 50 and SMA 200 with RVOL above 1.5 in either direction. The time stop checks closing profit against 2R.

        Bollinger bands use two population standard deviations. The flat-base scanner compares normalized ATR against the day’s market-wide 20th percentile and compares bandwidth with the previous session.

        Pullbacks use the 60-session half-life. Signals use the market close, with protective stops checked against the following sessions’ ranges.

            Execution assumptions

            Long-only. Signals at the close fill on the next available open. Slots are sized from prior equity and cash. Stop gaps fill at the open. Open positions are marked at the last saved close.

            Fees and slippage apply to both sides. Included cash dividends are credited on the ex-date and held as cash until used. Buy-and-hold starts at the first available open without trading costs. Sharpe uses daily returns and a zero risk-free rate.
